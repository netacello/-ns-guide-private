The Neurosteer Physician Guide

An interactive clinical reference for hdrEEG™ cognitive assessment — 13 sections, live graphs, full bibliography, and sample patient reports. All in one place.

Hi everyone,

I'm excited to share something I've been working on — an interactive, web-based Physician Guide for the Neurosteer assessment. It's designed as a living reference that physicians, researchers, and the team can open on any device, explore, and come back to. I collaborated with Claude (Anthropic's AI) to build and design it, keeping our brand colors, typography, and clinical language consistent throughout.

What's inside

13

Sections covering every neuromarker

Full clinical write-ups for NE (A0), TBR, BAR, ST4, VC9, T2, reaction time, Executive Function Age, brain age, sample reports, and neurofeedback protocols. Each section includes clinical interpretation, reference ranges, and what to tell the patient.
⬡

Live interactive charts — hover, zoom, filter

Key figures are fully interactive Plotly charts, not static images. Hover over a data point to see exact values, click legend entries to isolate groups, zoom in on specific ranges. The group separation plots, load profiles, cortisol correlations, and brain age scatter are all live.
+

Expandable scientific deep-dives

Every section has a collapsible Scientific Background block (click the + to expand). These go into the neuroscience — locus coeruleus–NE pathway, cortisol–EEG links, HRV validation, PD dopaminergic mechanism — without cluttering the main clinical view.
📄

Sample patient reports

The report view section shows exactly what a physician receives — the 42-point scoring table, flagged neuromarker gauge cards, clinical interpretation text, and the auto-generated neurofeedback protocol. Two full examples: healthy profile (34/42) and abnormal profile (20/42).
📚

Full publications library

A dedicated Publications page with all 13+ Neurosteer papers — 4 peer-reviewed journals (Frontiers, Scientific Reports, Nature), 4 IEEE conference papers, 3 arXiv preprints, and 3 manuscripts under review — sorted newest first, with expandable abstracts and DOI links. The page is sorted newest to oldest within each section.
📱

Fully mobile-friendly

The guide works beautifully on a phone — three-line hamburger menu in the top-left corner opens a slide-in navigation drawer. All sections, interactive charts, and expandable content work on mobile. I'll send the link to our WhatsApp group so you can check the phone view directly.

A note on the design: The guide was built and designed in collaboration with Claude (Anthropic). The visual language — paper-tone backgrounds, our brand red, the Inter/JetBrains Mono typographic pairing, the clinical section layout — was all designed to follow Neurosteer's identity. The interactive data layer uses Plotly.js, driven by real data from our full 977-participant dataset.

This is a living document — I'll keep updating it as new data, papers, and features come in. Feedback is very welcome.
